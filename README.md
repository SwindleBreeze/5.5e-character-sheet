# 5.5e Character Sheet

A free character builder and sheet for the fifth edition rules (2024), for phones and computers.
It works offline, installs like an app, and keeps everything on your device.

- **Build a character** step by step, with what each choice does explained as you go.
- **Play from the sheet:** every number explains itself, d20 rolls are a tap away, and the Combat
  tab puts a fight on one screen.
- **Level up, multiclass, take spells, buy gear** in the Shop, and keep notes.
- **Your data stays yours:** no account, no tracking, nothing uploaded. Back up to a file and
  restore it on another device.

## Content

The app works with content you provide:

- **A content pack** (`.pack.json.gz`) your group shares: the quickest way onto a phone.
- **Homebrew** in the 5etools JSON format, as files or links.
- **A data folder** of content you own in the 5etools JSON format, imported on a computer.

Import only what you own or have the right to use. Imported content never leaves your device.
